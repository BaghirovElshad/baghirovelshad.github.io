ABOUT ME

Professional Profile

I am a Geographic Information System (GIS) Specialist with a professional interest in geospatial data management, cartography and spatial analysis. My work centres on transforming geographic information into structured datasets and clear visual outputs that support practical understanding and decision-making.

I currently work as a GIS Specialist at Delta Clearing Solutions LLC, in the context of the Mine Action Agency of the Republic of Azerbaijan (ANAMA). This role connects my GIS work with the mine action field, where reliable geographic information and clear spatial communication are important.

I value accuracy, organised data workflows and thoughtful map design. I am interested in continuously developing my technical skills and contributing to projects where geospatial information can create practical value.
